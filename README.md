An interactive **3-page Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product trends, customer behavior, and regional performance. This project transforms sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and DAX-driven KPIs.

## 📌 Project Overview

The GrokMart Executive Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance, helping stakeholders monitor key metrics, identify sales trends, and make data-driven decisions.

The dashboard consists of three interactive pages:

1. **Executive Overview** – High-level business performance and key financial KPIs.
2. **Product Performance** – Product and category-wise sales and profitability analysis.
3. **Customer & Regional Analysis** – Customer segmentation, regional sales distribution, and performance comparison.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and business metrics
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, slicers, and drill-down analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview
- Consolidated view of sales, profit, orders, and units sold.
- KPI cards for monitoring overall business performance.
- Sales trend analysis over time.
- Interactive filtering for business-level insights.

### 2. Product Performance
- Product and category-wise sales analysis.
- Profitability comparison across product categories.
- Treemap and donut visualizations for contribution analysis.
- Identification of high-performing product categories.

### 3. Customer & Regional Analysis
- Customer and segment-level performance analysis.
- Regional sales comparison using map visualizations.
- Regional and category-wise performance breakdown.
- Interactive exploration of customer and geographic trends.

## 🧮 DAX Measures Implemented

Created DAX measures to calculate and track important business KPIs:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth %
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold

1. Clone or download this repository.
